1. What SetupLab does

For every car and track, SetupLab computes:

  • a qualifying setup: practice, track day, hotlap, time attack, qualifying;
  • a race setup: full stint, fuel, tyre wear, traffic. It starts from your validated qualifying setup.

It reads the car files (tyres, aero, engine, gearbox, brakes, suspension, differential, DRS, ERS) and the track's AI line, simulates the lap, searches for the best setup and explains every setting it proposes. It records your laps to fit its model to your driving, and refines the setup while you drive.

You stay in control: nothing is saved without your click, you keep or refuse each proposed effect yourself, and the car setup only changes when you confirm it, in the pits.

2. Installation

  1. Requirements: Assetto Corsa with Custom Shaders Patch 0.2.11 (the free version installed by Content Manager) or newer, paid previews included.
  2. Extract SetupLab-1.0.0-beta.1.5.zip into assettocorsa/apps/lua/: it already contains the SetupLab/ folder. In the end, assettocorsa/apps/lua/SetupLab/ holds SetupLab.lua and manifest.ini (no SetupLab folder inside another SetupLab). To update, replace the files: your settings and track memory are kept by CSP, outside this folder.
  3. In game, open SetupLab from the app bar or the setup screen. Two more windows exist: SetupLab Overlay (on track) and SetupLab Debug (diagnostics).

SetupLab works offline only: it pauses online and in replays.

3. Language

SetupLab is in English by default. The EN / FR badge at the top of the window switches language; the choice is also in the SETTINGS tab, and it is kept from one session to the next.

  • Everything follows the language: menus, buttons, messages, hover help, on-screen announcements, overlay and debug window. A message already on screen switches too.
  • The names of new files follow the language: SetupLab_Quali_v1.ini in English, SetupLab_Qualif_v1.ini in French. Files already written in the other language are still recognised (version numbers, track memory, tidying).

4. First launch

  1. Start an offline session and open SetupLab with the car in the pits: that is where it measures the reference ride height.
  2. Wait for "Discovery finished". The header shows the car, the track, the detected session, the data source and the model state (calibrated or not).
  3. The Step N/4 card, always at the top of the window, tells you what to do next, with the button for that step. Step 1: choose the setup to start from.

A setup changed in the menu, or a new session, restarts the discovery on its own.

5. The 4 steps

SetupLab refines the session setup: qualifying in practice, track day, hotlap, time attack and qualifying; race in a race session. The same four steps, with the same names, appear in the step card, the versions, the HELP tab and this guide; done in green, the next one in blue.

  1. StartChoose the setup SetupLab starts from: My current setup (the one in the car: yours, loaded from AC's setup menu, or the default one), Car's default setup (each setting at the car's default value, written to SetupLab_Default.ini and loaded in the pits; fuel, compound and the car script's own settings stay as they are), or Resume my reference vN (your validated setup on this track). Choosing activates SetupLab. A setup to load first is waited for: nothing is proposed before it is in the car.
  2. DriveAfter the minimum clean laps (see Minimum), SetupLab has the minimum information: it announces it once at the top of the screen ("v2 in preparation") and keeps analysing your laps while you stay on track. When you return to the pits, the version is announced as ready.
  3. TryTry v2 writes the proposed setup into a trial file (…_v2_trial.ini). In the pits: Load then Confirm, and drive at least one clean lap with it.
  4. ValidateValidate v2 keeps it as it is. To refuse one particular setting (for example the rear wing) and keep the rest: Modify… (see Validate or modify). The validated version becomes your reference for this track; load it and carry on: the refinement continues from it (step 2).
While a version is on trial, SetupLab proposes nothing else: validate it or start over first. A setup is only proposed if it gains at least 0.05 s per lap. Change the starting point, under the step card, starts again from another setup.

The SetupLab active box applies to this Assetto Corsa launch: at start-up, SetupLab is always on standby and writes nothing. On standby, it still analyses your laps. The starting point is chosen again at each new session.

6. Validate or modify

Under each version, one line sums up its changes ("7 settings changed"); all of them, with their old and new values and predicted gain, are on hover. Everything is kept by default: one click on Validate vN is enough.

  • Modify… opens the list, setting by setting, under the title of its family (aero, tyres, geometry, suspension, transmission, brakes, engine, aids). One click on Kept makes it Refused, and back. Keep everything cancels the refusals.
  • A refused setting keeps its old value in the validated version and in the next computations: it appears under "Your changes kept" and returns to the computation with Release your changes. The other settings of its family keep their new value.
  • Everything kept: the trial file becomes the version (renamed). Only some settings: a new file is written with the kept settings, and the trial file goes to the Recycle Bin once the car is stopped.
  • No setting kept: validation is refused; "Start over" to not keep the version.
  • Validate without trying saves the version right away, without the trial file.

7. Minimum before a proposal

At the top of the SETTINGS tab, "When SetupLab proposes a setup" decides when SetupLab may propose a setup or a change:

  • First setup right away, from the car and the track (unticked by default): ticked, SetupLab computes a first setup as soon as it is active, without waiting for a lap; the next versions wait for the minimum below;
  • clean laps before a proposal with the setup currently in the car, slider from 0 (right away) to 10 (2 by default);
  • minimum driving minutes (0 by default: none), on track, car moving.

Both must be reached. They are counted again after each load. Progress is shown in the step card and in the overlay ("minimum before a proposal: 1/2 clean laps").

9. Loading a setup into the car

Loading happens in the pits, car stopped, setup menu open. Next to the green button, SetupLab shows the file to load, the pit state (in the pits, car stopped, setup menu open, each in green or orange) and the reason for a refusal.

  1. Click Load the … setup into the car.
  2. Read the warning, then click Confirm the load before the time shown runs out.
  3. SetupLab first copies the current setup (SetupLab_Before_load_….ini, SetupLab_Avant_chargement_….ini in French), loads the file, then checks that the car has the expected setup. No copy is made when the car already has an intact setup kept by SetupLab.
Only a file written by SetupLab can be loaded: it recognises it by its name and the fingerprint of its content. A file changed since then is refused.

10. Your lap times

SetupLab relies on your real lap times:

  • your best lap with the current setup (in a race: your average lap);
  • estimated target: your time minus the predicted gain;
  • for each version: your laps with it, the predicted gain and the real gain. A partial version counts the laps driven with its full trial.

With at least 2 laps on each side and a difference above 0.15 s, SetupLab says whether a version is actually faster or slower. Times simulated by the model are on hover.

Only valid laps count: out laps, laps invalidated by AC, pit lane passes and off-tracks are left out (the reason is shown in the MEASURE tab).

11. In-game overlay

The SetupLab Overlay window opens from the CSP app bar. It has a translucent background and updates on every lap.

  • At the top, the verdict on your laps: the setup on track, your best lap with it and with the previous setup, then Better by … in green or Worse by … in red.
  • Your last lap and its difference with your best.
  • The changed settings that matter most, with the gain predicted by the model.
  • At the bottom, the next version when it is ready, or the minimum left before a proposal.

12. Your own changes in the pits

After a load by SetupLab, you can adjust the setup to your taste. SetupLab detects it at the next discovery and keeps each adjusted setting at your value in the next computations, like the refused effects. Release your changes gives them back to the computation. Fuel does not count as a change.

13. Track memory and lock

The setup you validate is kept, from one AC launch to the next, for this car on this track (layout included), per session (practice or track day, qualifying, race) and per track state (dry or wet). The MEMORY tab lists them and offers to load them, in two clicks, in the pits. A locked setup is no longer changed by the refinement; "Unlock" gives it back to the refinement.

14. Race setup

The race setup comes after a validated qualifying version (at least a v1): it starts from it, and your laps with it give your pace. Until then the RACE tab is locked and offers Go to the QUALIFYING tab. Then it shows three conditions, each with what fulfils it:

  1. Validated qualifying setup on this track (otherwise the setup validated in practice);
  2. in the car: Load the validated qualifying setup button;
  3. number of race laps, with the slider (Ctrl + click to type it), kept for this car on this track. The lap count planned by the session is shown as a reminder.

The computation then simulates the full stint: fuel load, tyre wear per axle, traffic (DRS in dirty air). The compound is only changed if wear has been measured.

15. Manual computation

Without automatic refinement, in the QUALIFYING or RACE tab: Compute the … setup (estimated duration shown beforehand, can be cancelled), read the proposed settings and their explanation on hover, Save the … setup (a new file on every click), then in the pits Load… and Confirm.

16. Rain

CSP rain, and Pure's rain, which goes through CSP, is read directly. As soon as the track gets wet, every computation proposes a wet setup: compound chosen between slicks, intermediates and wets; pressures for cooler tyres; wing and settings optimised with wet grip; stricter traction control and ABS. Wet laps calibrate a model separate from the dry one.

  • Version numbers continue from one session to the next on the same track, whatever the language.
  • The Setup name field replaces "SetupLab". Accents and spaces are removed; the kind is not repeated if your name already ends with it.
  • Your best lap is added to the end of the name of a kept qualifying version (SetupLab_Quali_v2_1.41.792.ini), with the car stopped, never while driving.
  • Never overwritten: a name already taken gets -2, -3…

Automatic tidying

With the car stopped, one file at a time, SetupLab sends to the Windows Recycle Bin the sorted trial files, the versions not kept, and the "before load" copies identical to a kept file.

SetupLab never touches your own files or the game's: only files it wrote during the session, intact. With CSP 0.2.11, the Recycle Bin is not yet available to apps: these files are then deleted permanently.

19. What SetupLab never does

  • Change the content of a file, or the game physics.
  • Touch your own files.
  • Write without your click: only "Try", "Save", "Validate", "Lock" and the backup copy before a load write a file.
  • Change the car setup without your confirmation in the pits.
  • Access the network, run online or in replays.

20. FAQ

I click "Load" and nothing happens.

Check the pit state shown above the button: you must be in the pits, car stopped, setup menu open. After the first click, confirm within the time shown.

SetupLab proposes nothing.

The step card says what is missing: the minimum clean laps or minutes (SETTINGS tab), a starting setup to load, a version on trial waiting to be validated, or, for the race, one of the three conditions in the RACE tab.

Why are some settings never changed?

The SETTINGS tab gives the reason for each on hover: transient effect outside the model, track roughness not measured yet, setting defined by the car's script…

How do I switch the app to French?

One click on the EN badge at the top of the window, or the SETTINGS tab, "Français".

How do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub with the report from the DIAGNOSTICS tab ("Copy the J0 report") and, if possible, the recorded lap (MEASURE tab, "Copy the recorded lap"). The copied report and lap are in French: that is expected.

21. Beta limitations

  • How closely the simulated lap matches AC still needs confirming on more cars: until calibration reaches its thresholds, the app shows "Low confidence: model not calibrated".
  • The rain model relies on assumptions; your wet laps calibrate it.
  • The copied J0 report and lap, meant for development, stay in French.
